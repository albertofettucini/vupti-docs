Vupti

Flick it to the phone next to you.

Photos, videos and files go straight from your iPhone to theirs. No account, no internet needed, nothing uploaded anywhere.

Get Vupti on the App Store

  • Just flick.Your latest photos sit as a stack of cards. Flick one up to send it. If a few phones are around, tap the one you want at the top first.
  • Phone to phone, encrypted.Files go from one iPhone to the other over Wi-Fi, encrypted end to end, and never through a server. Location data is removed from photos and videos before they leave.
  • You decide what comes in.A phone you don't know has to ask first. Phones you trust send straight through.
  • Free to use.Receiving is always free, and so is sending up to 100 MB in one flick. For bigger sends there's Vupti Pro, a one-time purchase.
